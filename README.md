# Sand Sprites

A rainbow sand-tray game for little kids, made for iPad and touch screens.

**Play:** https://ffserenity.github.io/sand-sprites/

## How to play
- **Draw in the sand** with a finger to push furrows and swirl the colours.
- **Hold still on the sand** to make a glowing whirlpool. A Sand Sprite pops out.
- **Find the peekers:** eyes sometimes peek out of the sand. Tap them!
- **Tap a sprite** to make it bounce and giggle (three taps = a spin).
- **Press and hold a sprite** to squish it like a squeeze toy, then let go.
- **Drag a sprite** to leave a coloured trail, or fling it to bounce off the walls.
- **Colours on the left** paint the sand (rainbow changes as you draw, ✋ just mixes).
- **🌙 Night** makes everything glow, **🌊 Smooth** washes in fresh sand, **👾 Summon** calls a sprite.

Every sprite has a material with its own feel and sound: jelly, squeaky, bouncy, glitter, bubble and fluffy.

## Install on iPad
Open the link in **Safari** → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It runs full-screen and works offline after the first visit.

All graphics and sounds are generated in code; there are no image or audio assets besides the app icon.
